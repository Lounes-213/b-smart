We are excited to introduce **b smart** (`bs`), a modern, high-performance workspace engineered for developer-agent collaboration.

Software engineering is undergoing a foundational shift. The era of simple autocomplete and standalone chat prompts is evolving into coordinated fleets of autonomous coding agents working in parallel. **b smart** was architected from the ground up to give developers complete visibility, control, and orchestration over this new paradigm.

## Why b smart?

Most AI tools force developers into rigid cloud silos or fragile browser extensions. **b smart** takes a different approach: it is local-first, privacy-focused, and endlessly adaptable.

Here is what makes **b smart** the ideal command center for your engineering workflows:

### 1. True Multi-Agent Orchestration

In **b smart**, you aren't limited to a single AI provider or a single thread. Run **Claude Code**, **OpenAI Codex**, **Cursor**, **Pi**, **Hermes**, and custom agents concurrently. Each agent operates in its own isolated Git worktree, preventing merge conflicts while allowing multiple features, bug fixes, and test suites to be built simultaneously.

### 2. Local-First & 100% Private

Your source code, secrets, environment variables, and branch history remain entirely on your machine. **b smart** connects directly to your local file system and your existing AI subscriptions — with zero middleman markups and zero vendor lock-in.

### 3. Mobile Companion Access (Android & iOS)

Step away from your desk without losing momentum. The **b smart mobile companion** lets you monitor agent progress, review diffs, answer questions, and dispatch new tasks whether you are on your local Wi-Fi or on 4G across the city. Scan the QR code in the desktop app to pair your phone in seconds.

### 4. Fully Extensible Architecture

Almost every surface in **b smart** can be customized or extended through plugins:

- **Custom Sidebars & Task Boards:** Manage issues and let agents update tickets in real time.
- **Automations & Scheduled Sweeps:** Set up agents to run nightly regression tests, review open PRs, or triage issues automatically.
- **Unified Terminal & Tooling:** Run long-running commands, dev servers, and live browser previews directly alongside your threads.

## The Future of Coding Is Here

**b smart** is open source and MIT-licensed. Whether you are an independent creator or building an engineering team, **b smart** gives you the foundation to code smarter, move faster, and build without limits.
